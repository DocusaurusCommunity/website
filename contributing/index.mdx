Found a useful plugin, spotted a mistake, or learned something that might help someone else? Contributions are welcome.

- [Add a plugin](./plugins.mdx) to the directory, either through the submission form or a pull request.
- Improve a guide or add a new article in the [knowledge base](/knowledge/). Its Markdown and MDX files live in `docs/`.
- Read about the [site's custom functionality](./customisations.mdx) before changing its components or theme.

Work happens in the [DocusaurusCommunity/website repository](https://github.com/DocusaurusCommunity/website). The [README](https://github.com/DocusaurusCommunity/website#installation) covers local setup and validation.
